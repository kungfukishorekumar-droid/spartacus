asset you own. Everything below is written to match the website, `llms.txt` and the
blog **word for word** — answer engines cross-reference sources, and inconsistent
descriptions weaken all of them.

## 6. Photos — 30+, refreshed monthly

Photo freshness correlates with Map Pack performance, and these double as blog and
Instagram assets.

- **Exterior** (3–4): entrance, signage, the street approach people will look for
- **Interior** (5–6): training floor, matted area, equipment, the space from the door
- **Team** (3–4): Kishore coaching, correcting a student, mid-demonstration
- **Classes in action** (10+): kids batch, adults batch, women's self-defence, pad work,
  forms — vary the angle and time of day
- **Credibility** (5+): medals, certificates, competition photos, judging appointments,
  gradings
- **Students** (5+): group photos, belt presentations — ⚠️ get parental consent before
  posting any child's face

⚠️ Never post a stock photo here. Google's own guidance and local searchers both
punish it.

## 7. Reviews — the engine that actually moves rankings

Review **velocity and recency** matter more than raw count. A steady 3–5 per month
beats 40 in one week (which also looks manipulated).

**When to ask:** after a grading, after a first month, after a competition, after a
workshop. Not at random.

**WhatsApp template:**

```
Hi [Name], really glad [Student] is enjoying training at Spartacus 🙏

If you have a minute, a short Google review genuinely helps other Chennai parents
find us — it's the main way people choose an academy.

[YOUR GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

Thank you!
— Coach Kishore
```

Get the short link from GBP → Ask for reviews → Share review form.

**Reply to every review within 48 hours.** Thank positives by name and reference
something specific. For negatives: respond calmly in public, never argue, offer to
resolve it offline. A well-handled negative review builds more trust than a wall of
five stars.

⚠️ Never buy, incentivise or write reviews. It risks the whole listing, and it is the
one mistake that cannot be undone.
