Quick Answers About Spartacus Martial Arts Academy

Short, direct answers for parents, students, and anyone searching for martial arts classes in Chennai.

What is Spartacus Martial Arts Academy?

Spartacus Martial Arts Academy is a Chennai-based martial arts academy by Kishore Kumar offering Wushu, Kung Fu, Karate, Judo, Kick Boxing, Boxing, self-defense, fitness, and discipline-based training.

Who is Coach Kishore Kumar?

Kishore Kumar is a National Wushu Medalist, Kungfu Black Belt, Wushu Coach & Judge, Martial Artist, and Sports Psychology-based Athlete Mindset Coach.

What martial arts are taught?

The academy offers Wushu, Kung Fu, Karate, Judo, Kick Boxing, and Boxing training in Chennai.

Can kids join?

Yes, kids and students can join beginner-friendly martial arts classes with safe and structured coaching.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes, beginners can start with foundation training, basic fitness, stance, movement, discipline, and simple techniques.

How can I book a trial class?

You can book a free trial class by filling the website form or sending a WhatsApp message to Coach Kishore at 9884599939.
