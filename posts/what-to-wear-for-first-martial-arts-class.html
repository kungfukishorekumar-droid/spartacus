What to Wear for Your First Martial Arts Class

No uniform needed for day one. Here is exactly what to wear and bring for your first martial arts class — and what to skip.

Spartacus Martial ArtsBy Kishore Kumar18 Sept 2022 · 09:20 PMUpdated 13 Oct 20265 min read
What to Wear for Your First Martial Arts Class

Quick Answer

For your first class: comfortable t-shirt, track pants or shorts, and a water bottle. Train barefoot on mats. No uniform needed until you decide to continue. Skip jewellery and heavy meals.

The question every beginner asks the night before: "What do I wear?" Relax — day one is simpler than you think. Here is exactly what to wear, what to bring, what to skip, and how to prepare.

Wear this

  • A comfortable t-shirt you can sweat in. Fitted is better than very loose, which can catch fingers and toes.
  • Track pants or sports shorts — anything that allows a full leg swing. Avoid zips, buttons and belts.
  • Bare feet — most martial arts are trained barefoot on mats. Some fitness or boxing classes may use training shoes; check when you book.
  • Hair tied back securely, without metal clips.
  • Short, clean nails — to protect yourself and your partners.

Bring this

  • A water bottle — essential in Chennai's heat.
  • A small towel.
  • Slippers or sandals for walking to and from the mat area.
  • Any medication you might need, such as an inhaler — and tell the coach.

Skip this

  • Jewellery, watches, rings and earrings — they scratch you and your partners and can catch. If something cannot be removed, cover it with tape.
  • Heavy meals within about 90 minutes of class. A light snack an hour or two before is fine.
  • Jeans. Please. Every academy has a jeans story.
  • Strong perfume — you will be training close to others.

For parents of young children

Dress your child in comfortable sports clothes, take them to the toilet before class, and pack a water bottle with their name on it. Arrive a little early so they can see the space and meet the coach calmly. Read how to prepare your child for the first class.

For women

A sports bra and a t-shirt that stays in place during kicks and floor work are the most comfortable. Leggings or track pants work well. Wear whatever helps you feel comfortable and able to move freely.

Arrive ready

  • Arrive 10 minutes early to settle in and meet the coach.
  • Tell the coach about any injuries, health conditions or concerns.
  • Drink water through the day, not just at class.
  • Come with an open mind — you are not expected to know anything yet.

For what to expect in your first weeks, read the first 30 days of martial arts.

When does the uniform come?

After you decide to continue. The academy will guide you to the right uniform for your art:

  • Karate — a lightweight gi and belt.
  • Judo — a thicker, reinforced judogi built to be gripped.
  • Kungfu and Wushu — a training uniform suited to the style.
  • Kickboxing and Boxing — often sports clothing or an academy t-shirt.

What about gloves and gear?

Do not buy gear before your first class. Depending on your art, you may later need hand wraps, gloves, shin guards and a mouthguard. Ask the coach which sizes and types to buy, so you do not waste money on the wrong equipment. Our fees guide explains what to budget for.

Are uniforms compulsory later?

For most traditional arts, yes, once you are training regularly. A uniform shows commitment, helps the coach see your posture and techniques, and is part of the training culture.

Day one is about feeling the training, not looking the part. book a free trial class — dress comfortably, arrive curious.
